The immutable direction passes the disabled-tiering independent gate and wins seven external comparison cells. It retains five external losses, two construction losses to the existing mutable tree, and an unresolved ordinary-tiering guard failure. An explicitly per-operation best-of-Tedd portfolio wins nine external cells; it is not one implementation or a validated automatic dispatch policy, and still loses three small-tree cells. All proposed library changes remain in `specialization.patch`; original production files are unchanged. Applying the patch requires a concrete scope/tradeoff decision plus the source-write authorization demanded by automatic approval review.
